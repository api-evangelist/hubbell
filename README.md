# Hubbell (hubbell)

Hubbell is a global manufacturer of high-quality electrical and electronic products for non-residential and residential construction, industrial, and utility applications. The company has provided trusted infrastructure solutions since 1888 across power and utilities, lighting and controls, wiring and electrical, and datacom product categories serving commercial, data center, industrial, healthcare, telecom, and water and gas markets.
